Privacy Policy

AI Transfer rclone
Last updated: October 7, 2026

Purpose

AI Transfer rclone is a private, single-user workflow that synchronizes files between the user's Google Drive and devices or servers controlled by the same user.

Google user data

The application accesses Google Drive data only after the user grants permission. Access is used solely to list, read, create, and update files required for the user's requested synchronization within a user-designated transfer folder.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are stored in the user's own environment. Google Drive data is transferred only between Google Drive and systems controlled by the user. The application does not sell, advertise with, or disclose Google user data to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Files remain under the user's control and are retained until the user deletes them. The user may revoke the application's Google access at any time from the Google Account third-party connections settings and may delete local credentials and synchronized files.

Security

The workflow uses Google's OAuth authorization and rclone. Access credentials are not intentionally published or included in this website.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be submitted through the GitHub repository that hosts this website.
